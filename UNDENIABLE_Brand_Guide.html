01Brand

What the identity carries

Undeniable makes heavyweight garment-dyed essentials in Texas — one spec, four capsules a year, each cut once. The identity has one job: make a plain black t-shirt read as equipment, not merch.

Four traits
  • 01Specified, not styled. Numbers before adjectives.
  • 02Finite. Cut once. Serialised, dated, closed.
  • 03Quiet at scale. The wordmark appears once on a garment, small.

02Logo

Seven points, one axis, one void

The mark is a seven-point burst — not an asterisk, and not radially even. Three measured facts define it, and all three must survive any redraw.

Fig. 2.1 — Anatomy · tip angle / radius as a fraction of Rmax
Fact 01

Mirror, not rotation

Points pair across the vertical axis to within 0.1°. Flip one half — never rotate a petal around the center.

Fact 02

The 85° void

Twelve o’clock is empty. Every other gap is 41–52°. That opening stops it reading as a star.

Fact 03

Weighted upward

Radii step down as points descend — 1.00, 0.92, 0.80, 0.74. Equalising them kills the mark.

03Space & size

A third of A on every side

The exclusion zone is A/3 — the same measurement as the gap inside the lockup, so the logo never sits tighter to its neighbors than to itself.

Fig. 3.1 — Exclusion zone · A/3 on all four sides

Mark minimum — 24px

In practice
  • 01Measure from the bounding box, not the outermost point tip.
  • 02Page edges count as neighbors.
  • 03On imagery the zone must be visually clear too — no seams or faces.
  • 04More is always fine. A/3 is a floor, not a target.
Embroidery

The lockup is not approved for embroidery at any size — the wordmark counters fill in before the mark reaches its own minimum. Use the mark alone at 15mm or larger.

07Brackets

Brackets mean something

The corner bracket is a viewfinder mark: this is selected, live, or yours to act on. Used that way it is unmistakable. Used as decoration it becomes wallpaper by the second season.

Fig. 7.1 — Arm is five times stroke · at 1.5px stroke the arm is 8–12px
PropertyValue
Stroke (T)1.5 px · 0.35 mm
Arm5T — 8 / 10 / 12 px
ColorInherits from context
CornersAll four, or none
RadiusNone. Square ends.
MotionFade + scale 0.7→1, 350 ms on hover
Three positions
Flush
Flush — on inkSitting on the border. The default: panels, cards, fields.
Inset
Inset — on fillsHeld inside. For imagery and solid color with no border of their own.
Outset
Outset — on paperFloating outside. One object per screen, maximum.
Bracket this
  • ✓Things you can act on — product cards, buttons, tiles.
  • ✓Things that are live — timers, stock warnings, drops.
  • ✓Things holding hard data — spec tables, size charts.
  • ✓The current selection — chosen size or color.
Never bracket
  • ×The logo. It has its own clear space.
  • ×Body copy or headlines. Brackets mark objects, not sentences.
  • ×Three corners. Four or none.
  • ×Nested panels or mixed arm lengths in one row.
08Application

One mark, one code, nothing else

A garment carries the identity twice: the stacked lockup at the left chest and the product code down a sleeve or hem. That is the entire allowance.
